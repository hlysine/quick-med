Estimate creatinine clearance (CrCl) from serum creatinine (µmol/L), age, sex and body weight

- Cockcroft & Gault formula
  - `CrCl = (140 - age) * weight / (72 * SCr)` in mL/min
  - Multiply by 0.85 for females
  - SCr in mg/dL (µmol/L / 88.4), weight in kg
- Salazar-Corcoran formula (for obese patients, requires body height)
  - Male: `CrCl = (137 - age) * (0.285 * weight + 12.1 * height^2) / (51 * SCr)`
  - Female: `CrCl = (146 - age) * (0.287 * weight + 9.74 * height^2) / (60 * SCr)`
  - SCr in mg/dL (µmol/L / 88.4), weight in kg, height in m
- Ideal body weight (IBW)
  - Male: `50 + 0.91 * (height - 152.4)` in kg
  - Female: `45.5 + 0.91 * (height - 152.4)` in kg
  - Height in cm
  - If total body weight / IBW ratio ≥ 1.9, consider Salazar-Corcoran formula
